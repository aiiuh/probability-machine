# Primary-source research ledger

Reviewed 19 September 2026. Publication dates are not retrieval dates. Entries support bounded claims; they do not certify this implementation or prove a universal behavior theory. No third-party source text or dataset is redistributed.

- **R1 — Silver, Singh, Precup & Sutton (2021), Reward is enough.** Artificial Intelligence, DOI 10.1016/j.artint.2021.103535. Authors' hypothesis about reward maximization and intelligent abilities; not a universal human-prediction theorem. [Paper](https://sites.rutgers.edu/critical-ai/wp-content/uploads/sites/586/2022/01/Silver_Reward-is-Enough.pdf). PDF opening pages and reward-hypothesis discussion inspected, including page screenshots.
- **R2 — Vamplew et al., Scalar reward is not enough.** [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.15422); [published paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10458-022-09575-5). Counterargument motivating multiobjective representations. It does not show that no conceivable scalar representation can encode behavior.
- **R3 — Skalse & Abate (2024), Partial Identifiability and Misspecification in Inverse Reinforcement Learning.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15951). Formal reward ambiguity and behavioral-model misspecification. Applies under its formal models, not a blanket denial of useful inference.
- **R4 — Cao, Cohen & Szpruch (2021), Identifiability in inverse reinforcement learning.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.03498). Additional environments or discount conditions can improve reward identification under stated assumptions. Does not imply that arbitrary human motives are recoverable from ordinary click logs.
- **R5 — Putkonen et al. (2025), Understanding Visual Search in Graphical User Interfaces.** [Institutional record](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/items/8f801308-7d11-4645-913e-d21b8e076856); [authors' code](https://github.com/aalto-ui/VSGUI10K); [linked data](https://osf.io/hmg9b/). Suitable candidate for empirical GUI-search evaluation. Data retrieval was blocked here; no dataset benchmark was run. Verify current data license and contents before importing.
- **R6 — Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski (2006), The Motivational Pull of Video Games: A Self-Determination Theory Approach.** [Published paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11031-006-9051-8). Studies of competence, autonomy, relatedness, control experience, and enjoyment. Associations do not create a universal engagement objective or predict every player.
- **R7 — Dudik, Langford & Li (2011), Doubly Robust Policy Evaluation and Learning.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.4601). Basis for contextual-bandit counterfactual evaluation. Requires assumptions and support; our implementation does not provide inference for clustered/adaptive data.
- **R8 — Probably language demonstration.** [Project](https://probably-lang.southpolesteve.workers.dev/). Semantic/probabilistic authoring concept. Current demonstration availability is not evidence of a production durable runtime. Source/license and exact semantics require review before adoption.
- **R9 — TypeSafe primitives.** [Documentation](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives). Typed semantic decisions and explicit probability distributions. Useful potential annotation/provider interface, not measured causal truth.
- **R10 — TypeSafe confidence.** [Documentation](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence). Confidence is a documented distribution-derived statistic. Keep it separate from the winning probability and empirical calibration. No live API benchmark was run.
- **R11 — W3C, Understanding WCAG 2.2 Target Size (Minimum).** [Official guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html). Accessibility requirements and exceptions are separate constraints, not an optional low-weight utility metric. The browser lab is not certified compliant.
- **R12 — Kaelbling, Littman & Cassandra (1998), Planning and acting in partially observable stochastic domains.** [Paper](https://people.csail.mit.edu/lpk/papers/aij98-pomdp.pdf). Conceptual reference for uncertain state and information actions. The implemented solver is a fully observed finite MDP solver, not a POMDP implementation.

## Evidence rules

Record source, claim, scope, method, population, limitations, and whether a number is observed, simulated, assumed, or model-generated. A bibliography entry is not a substitute for reading the relevant method before implementation. Provider claims, performance demonstrations, and generated-agent agreement are not independent human ground truth.
